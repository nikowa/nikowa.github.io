Status: Released

Royal Dispute is a visual novel with a typing mini-game. You play as a Destined Prince or a Demon King and get to romance a very busy princess. I made this for a month-long game jam in 2026, with a team of 6 people.

The game was made in Godot, which I’d never used before, but I learned very quickly. I programmed the visual novel part of the game.

I spent half the time implementing the dialogue system and half the time doing effects and animations, to make it look as nice and polished as possible.

Status: WIP

Soap Glider is a precision platformer inspired by the classic Android game Run. It’s an infinite runner (a forward scroller) where you steer with the mouse and try not to fall off the platforms. You can also do spins in the air. I called it Soap Glider because my younger brother saw me playing it and noted that the character looked like a soap.

I worked on this for 11 months in 2024/2025, then I put it on the shelf temporarily, to work on smaller things until I have enough experience.

This was the first game I made that used ray-tracing. It was also the first game that was fun enough that I think I could play it for hours.
